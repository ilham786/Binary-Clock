⚡ Important Notes:
• Add a 10µF capacitor across VCC and GND on the MAX7219 module to prevent power glitches.
• The DS3231 uses 3.3V (not 5V). Connecting to 5V will damage it.
• Set the HARDWARE_TYPE in the sketch to FC16_HW for common eBay modules, or GENERIC_HW for bare modules.
• The RTC auto-sets to compile time on first upload if the CR2032 battery is new.
